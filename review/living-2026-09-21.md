# Living-area repairs and refinement — 2026-09-21

Baseline: main `844e97624bd9a132c9f93c7d1a13716c107cc2d9`. Model work authored in Blender 4.5.14 LTS; local packed reconstruction is `source/home-deco-living.blend`. Reproduction: [source README](../source/README.md).

## Changes and causes

- Laundry niches: opposing coincident liner triangles and lining coplanar with the structural column produced striped depth fighting. Remove redundant faces and give the remaining lining 1.5 mm clearance into the recess; retain the structural column and the two openings.
- Sofa rug: woven binding tops exactly coincided with the rug top at y≈0.015 m. Raise binding 1.5 mm. No global polygon offset or depth-test bypass.
- Entry mirror: waterproof facing front and shared wall both lay at z≈4.775511 m; the curved enclosure cap overlapped the same plane. Offset the facing 2 mm toward the room and remove the buried cap. Mirror remains the existing non-reflecting placeholder, as previously requested; no new real-time reflection pass.
- Trees: 380,180 → 135,356 triangles (64.4% fewer), from the explicitly approved current baseline. Non-destructive Blender Decimate modifiers: branches/leaves 0.35, trunk 0.5. Retain three shared mesh prototypes across four instances. Exterior download 1,271,552 → 865,984 bytes. Full original high-resolution tree authoring source is unavailable.
- Table arrangement: three asymmetric curved stems with tapered tips, attached petioles and folded, variably sized double-sided leaves. Original hollow vase and water retained; no extra transmissive draw calls. Colored vertices and curvature replace uniformly flat-looking leaf silhouettes.
- Books: individually varied dimensions, depth, inclination, horizontal stacks and geometric spine lettering combined into existing meshes. Original color family retained, explicit exported vertex colors. Existing shelf layout and ceramics remain.
- Master desk: remove the side chair (including its hidden legacy parts); retain the chair facing the long desktop. Preserve empty hierarchy nodes and stable attachment indices.
- Cabinets: the runtime previously overwrote authored ivory with linear RGB (0.60,0.59,0.56), close to the plaster. Remove that override and the generic white-darkening adjustment for explicit lacquer IDs. Reduce grain amplitude 0.12 → 0.025 mm, roughness 0.43 → 0.32 and roughness noise 0.20 → 0.045. Plaster stays matte; exposure/lights are not globally boosted. AO/lighting shader composition is retained.

## Validation

- 1,480 mesh nodes / 12 modules, glTF validator **0 errors**; exact decoded geometry/index equality with assembled final bake source, original transforms and metadata retained.
- 136 edited meshes checked against Blender world bounds within 2 mm; vertex colors present, rug clearance >1 mm. Both bedding modules byte-identical to baseline.
- Final total 606,721 triangles vs 876,161 before this revision. All model, shared texture, lighting, finish and fixture resources total **8,655,920 bytes**, below 20 MB; initial geometry/textures 3,476,224 bytes.
- All three lighting stages re-baked from final assembled geometry. Old unused hashed outputs removed. Budget/version check and loading-progress checks pass; production Vite build succeeds (existing vendor-chunk size advisory only).
- Same-camera baseline/candidate Chrome captures cover niches, rug, bookshelf, table, entry mirror, master desk and wardrobe. Browser report: [living-browser.json](living-browser.json). Screenshots and minor camera offsets are functional/visual checks, not a claim of user-device FPS or physically accurate GI. No change to collision-free walking or the startup progress logic.

During integration, per-node books initially exposed a shared primitive aliasing bug; mesh and primitive cloning plus world-bounds checks prevented it from shipping. Blender's default MATERIAL color export omitted unused vertex colors; explicit NAME export fixes that. These intermediate attempts are not production versions.
