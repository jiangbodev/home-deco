# Warm-white three-seat sofa

The user asked for a lighter, slightly longer sofa, then explicitly chose three seat cushions while retaining the 2.32 m length. The source checkpoint is `038122d`; the exact modular geometry at that checkpoint is also the prior lossless `qa/realism-baseline/source.glb`.

## Authored change

- Blender 4.5.14 LTS: widen the existing sofa group from 2.20 to 2.32 m about its center. Height, depth and position remain unchanged. Frame, feet, cushions, pillows and existing shadow mesh stay aligned.
- Rebuild the seat as three connected-component copies of the original tailored cushion, each about 0.611 m wide, with 12 mm gaps. Preserve source UVs/material texture, smooth normals and the existing single seat mesh/draw. Retain two decorative pillows.
- Lift the upholstery base texture toward warm white with a 40% authored mix; keep woven variation, normal/roughness maps and shaded folds. Three upholstery materials share one WebP encoded at quality 92 from the Blender-edited image. Do not raise whole-room exposure or make fabric emissive.
- Side clearances to the adjacent cabinet and structural column are 36.6 and 39.0 mm. Other 1,475 mesh bounds remain byte-exact in the assembled validation. Scene remains 1,480 meshes; triangles 606,721 → 611,421 (+4,700, 0.775%). The extra seat does not add another mesh or draw call.

## Verification

- `scripts/check-sofa.mjs`: exact width, three independent 4,700-triangle seat islands, both 11.98 mm decoded gaps, side clearance, unchanged other mesh bounds, and decoded glTF validation (0 errors). Raw records in `sofa-blender.json`, `sofa-integration.json`, `sofa-geometry.json`.
- All three fallback contact/daylight/object bakes rerun from the new assembled modules. Cycles diffuse atlases and both HDR probes also regenerated from that exact source; no metadata-only version substitution.
- Browser comparison uses the same front, rug, niche and other room cameras against `038122d`, with complete modules and optional lighting loaded. Eight same-camera views: no shader/page errors; draw-call counts unchanged. [Comparison](sofa-comparison.jpg), [browser data](sofa-browser.json). Production build and full resource budget/version checks passed. Complete resources: **12,820,334 bytes**, +21,807 bytes versus the prior release; entry model/texture resources about 3.49 MB. No new runtime rendering pass or JavaScript was added. This change has not been separately benchmarked on a physical phone.

## Reproduction

Run `blender-refine-sofa.py` from the immutable pre-edit lossless source, not an already-widened output, then `import-blender-sofa.mjs` using immutable original modules. The local packed `source/home-deco-sofa.blend` remains editable. `blender-realistic-lighting.py -- SOURCE OUTPUT --sofa-only` creates a focused Cycles reference without rendering unrelated views; reassemble and rebake all production lighting afterward.
